## Unreleased (after v0.4.31; no version set)

- **Changed, may need action:** a governed write now needs the requesting agent's **own stored authority** as well as a person's approval (`no_authority` otherwise). It is checked when proposed, when decided and just before the change, so **revoking an agent also stops its waiting requests**. Connections made by OAuth and tokens issued with `--authority-db` already have it. A deployment with `AGENTIC_OS_WRITES_DIR` but no `AGENTIC_OS_AUTHORITY_DB`, or file tokens issued without `--authority-db`, can no longer propose changes: set the store and reconnect or reissue. This is the same envelope the gateway already enforces in the production profile; no second permission system was added.
- **Fix:** changing a record of an installed application deleted the stored values of any field a newer version had dropped. They are now kept, and the card of a newer version says what it hides or restricts for records already kept.
- Installed applications can be moved with their records: a person who may decide exports one from its page and brings it into another tenant or deployment after reviewing it in words (`aos.app-bundle/v1`; all or nothing, never overwriting; unsigned, so only from a source you trust).

- The card for installing an application now says in words what approving does, what each tool gives access to (the platform's description, then the author's reason marked as unchecked), what may change in other applications, the permissions an agent needs, what is kept and who sees it. The exact definition, which was the first row as raw text, is folded underneath.

## v0.4.29 (governed media, social and market research; a safe payment correction)

- Economic intelligence: one deposit smaller than the payment no longer excludes a partial settlement until the statement runs past the time a second instalment could arrive; it is reported as unknown.
- **Fix:** the economics tools offered to agents now read ERPNext child tables with their parent. With an API user that may not list a child table directly, reconciliation could not be read at all, and ERPNext can also answer such a read with names only, which left invoice links and discounts out.
- Governed payment correction (`economics.request_payment_correction`, grant `economics.propose`), **off by default**. A bank difference alone cannot trigger it: the operator's own statement export must corroborate it and rule out a fee, currency, instalment, allocation, reversal and timing. The approver sees the evidence and attests to it; the original voucher is kept and amended; the ledger is read back; a half-done correction is finished only after a new approval, without a duplicate. See docs/AGENT_GATEWAY_EXTERNAL_AGENTS.md.
- Market research for external agents (`market.*`) over Market Radar's operator and the Mission Runtime: research missions on operator-listed sites only, evidence behind every recommendation, and two follow-ups a person approves (watch a page; a change plan bound to the list's digest), then checked against the live site. No new mission engine. See docs/AGENT_GATEWAY_EXTERNAL_AGENTS.md.
- Approvals can require the approver's attestation (`attested` on the decision; a box on the approval page).
- Video, images and social posts for external agents: `media.*` over VibeXGen's operator and `social.*` over Postiz's public API. Editing and export make new files with ffmpeg; model generation and every Postiz change are proposals a person approves. Files belong to the tenant that made them, plans are a closed list of steps, only an exported (and, where a model had a part, labelled) file can be posted, the approval is bound to the file's SHA-256, and there is no publish-now. See docs/AGENT_GATEWAY_EXTERNAL_AGENTS.md.

## v0.4.24 (governed writes with approval pages; explicit authorization; economic events)

- **Approval pages in Projects** (`/approvals`): sign-in for approvers and one list for governed writes, Edge Sentinel actions and quality runs, with cards showing the requesting agent, tenant, application, exact changes, affected records, expiry and outcome. A proposal returns `approval_url`, a deep link that carries no credential. Decisions go through the existing gates and require the same permissions as the APIs.
- Governed writes: every lookup and proposal is **permitted, approval-required or denied** (`not_authorized (<code>): ...`); an approval never overrides a denial, checked at proposal, approval and execution. Replaces the permission-seam exemption: with permissions on, the plane is gated as `governed-writes`. A change nobody could approve is refused. A proposal may not contain the reserved marker `aos-approval`.
- **Changed:** a financial lookup without `egress:financial` returns a `missing_grant` error (it answered `pending_approval`, which nothing could approve).
- **Changed:** separation of duties is **per tenant**, and adding an approver no longer leaves requesters unidentified (their reads lost their tenant, and a Today approval was recorded as `user:operator`). A requester stops deciding only when its own tenant has an approver.
- A person's product decision is refused for a product their tenant is not granted (`products:<id>`).
- Economic events (`agentic_os/economics`, [docs/ECONOMIC_EVENTS.md](docs/ECONOMIC_EVENTS.md)): a read-only projection of ERPNext's own documents into typed events (sales at list price, discounts with the approval claim written on the document (an unverified comment, shown as such, never as proof of approval), credit notes, cancellations, supplier bills, support, rework and cancellation-handling time), each marked **posted** or **estimated** and tied to its source records; no second ledger. Answers: profit per customer, cancellation cost, discount loss by approver, supplier cost, and payments the bank does not confirm (traced, with a correction that is proposed and never applied) with the history of payments cancelled and entered again. `verify.ledger_check` compares the events with the general ledger by a separate path. `reliability.measure` is the measured, independently verified reliability of an operation class (a measurement; it decides nothing). New `economics` dashboard, shown in Projects and to agents holding `dashboards.read`.
- Fixed: ERPNext 16 answers a child-table read without `parent=<doctype>` with rows that hold only `name`; the ERPNext client now sends it (checked on ERPNext 16.53 for invoice items; the purchase-order and receipt line reads use the same call), and `read_list` raises where `get_list` returns an empty list. An absent column in an answer is now a recorded gap, not a zero.
- Agent gateway: ERPNext, support and CRM for external agents behind named grants: record lookups, and **governed writes** an agent only proposes (comment, draft purchase order, private note, add-if-absent company or contact). Each waits in the Mission Runtime's approval gate for a person of its tenant (`/api/writes`, `writes:decide`), runs once and is read back; an unknown outcome is never re-sent. Off until configured (`AGENTIC_OS_WRITES_DIR`, `app:<name>` per tenant).
- Fixed: egress redaction masked parts of some UUID record ids (its card-number pattern).
- Fixed: the Twenty writer created a contact when its duplicate lookup failed (a failed lookup was read as "no such person").

## v0.4.13 (behind the gate nothing is publicly cacheable; the QA model is bounded)

- **Security fix for 0.4.12**: behind the sign-in gate the UI's hashed files are `Cache-Control: private` (the person's own browser may keep them). 0.4.12 marked them `public`, so an edge cache in front of a gated deployment served the script and stylesheet to anonymous callers without asking the server. No data was exposed (the files are the public UI bundle), but the gate's contract is that nothing is served before sign-in. Deployments on 0.4.12 behind a CDN should upgrade.

## v0.4.1 (Agent Gateway idempotent replay; date set when tagged)

- **Agent Gateway (idempotency):** a stored idempotent result is now served as a call. The caller must still see the capability (grants, OAuth scope) and, with an authority guard, still be inside its authority (envelope and child-agent revocation, child credential, expiry), checked without reserving or consuming the call allowance; the egress policy is asked again about the stored output. An idempotency key is bound, per tenant, to a fingerprint of workspace, subject, authority key, capability and the canonical arguments: reuse with a different request returns the new client code `idempotency_conflict`, and a call still running under the key returns `idempotency_in_progress`; concurrent retries execute once. A side-effecting capability is covered whether or not it is declared `idempotent`, so a retry of a write with a key (also after `reconcile_call(executed=True)`) replays the stored result instead of sending again. A write whose output the egress policy holds for export approval is stored as held, so a retry replays it and does not send again; the egress policy is re-applied to a stored output when its action has become higher-ranked. `InMemoryIdempotencyStore` has a TTL (24 hours) and an entry bound (10,000, shared by tenants; completed reads of the tenant holding the most entries are evicted first; optional `max_entries_per_tenant`); it never evicts an in-flight claim or the completed entry of a write before its TTL, and a keyed call that finds no room fails with `gateway_error`. A write can be sent again after its entry expires, after a restart or on a second worker with its own store; use a durable shared store in production. Replays and conflicts are audited without arguments or results; a fault while revalidating is `gateway_error`. The `IdempotencyStore` protocol is now `begin(tenant, key, fingerprint, write=)`/`complete`/`abandon`/`seed` (a custom store whose `begin` lacks `write=` makes every keyed covered call fail with `gateway_error`; a durable store persists the unfiltered output of a held write for the TTL and must be protected accordingly); a gateway given a store without them raises `TypeError` when it is built. New module `agentic_os/agent_gateway/idempotency.py`, new `AuthorityGuard.check`. Behaviour table and impact: `docs/PERMISSIONS_MIGRATION.md` section 34. Builds on #586.
- **Root `Dockerfile` builds again:** it now copies `hatch_build.py` (the wheel build hook named in `pyproject.toml`) and skips the Projects UI download for this image, which serves the control plane only. The image at the `v0.4.0` tag does not build. `tests/test_root_dockerfile.py` checks statically that every file the install needs is copied and not excluded by `.dockerignore`. See `docs/PERMISSIONS_MIGRATION.md` section 33.

See `docs/PERMISSIONS_MIGRATION.md` section 30.
- **Permissions (key file):** `agentic-os permissions init` no longer creates `<dir>/.permissions.key` when `PERMISSIONS_KEY` is set (the grants were already sealed with the environment key; the file was an unused second key beside the ciphertext). A key file next to an environment key is warned about once, by path only, and never used. `init` honours `PERMISSIONS_KEY_FILE` and refuses to create a key file under `PERMISSIONS_REQUIRE_KEY=1`. See `docs/PERMISSIONS_MIGRATION.md` section 32.

Enterprise SSO / tenant identity / advanced permissioning / permission-aware retrieval and cache requested without the package fail closed with a missing-capability error. Ten more apps (control-tower, document-editor, growth-assistant, guide, infra, outreach-engine, social-autopilot, agentic-crm, lifecycle, support) enforce grants where their routes and operator capabilities execute.

- Sources/RAG: `RagIndexer` could give different (project, source) pairs the same DuckDB index file (`("a_b","c")` and `("a","b_c")`; likewise with `:`), the same in-memory store, and an id such as `../escape` could place an index outside the index directory: retrieval across projects. With it the probe identifies the stored message by its own header instead of treating a lookalike as a candidate. The caller must set it: nothing sets it automatically yet.

- Edge Sentinel SOC 2 evidence (AVL-BCK-01): `AzurePostgresBackups` collects completed backups from Azure Database for PostgreSQL Flexible Server (a window with none listed is a gap, never a finding or a pass; the `az` runner is limited to the one read-only listing) and `RestoreDrillLog` records the restore drills a person or job performs. Written from a real listing; a failed backup is invisible to this API and the limit is documented.
- Membrane: a sandbox asked for a memory ceiling on a platform that cannot apply one (macOS refuses `RLIMIT_AS`) now refuses by name (`memory_ceiling_unenforceable`) before anything runs, instead of dying in the child with an unexplained `SubprocessError`; it never runs a capability as if the ceiling applied, and a request for no ceiling (`max_memory_mb=0`) still runs. `membrane.memory_ceiling_enforceable()` reports whether the platform can.

- Goal loop and child agents: `GoalLoop(..., children=ChildSpawner)` keeps every active child under a mission's authority key alive (`ChildSpawner.keepalive_tree`, including children spawned while the mission runs and grandchildren) for exactly as long as the loop is driving that mission, and ends them (`ChildSpawner.end_tree`, deepest first, unused allowance returned) when the loop completes or is cancelled. A loop that only waits stops beating, so credentials lapse within one lease period and the same token works again when the loop resumes; a revoked or expired child is never revived. A child that lost its authority while the mission ran is recorded (`LoopChildLost`, `LOOP_CHILD_LOST` on the sentinel feed). Before this, nothing called `keepalive` or `close_tree`: a long mission's children lapsed mid-run and a finished loop left its children active until reaped. Without `children` nothing changes.

Found by the new SOC 2 assessment-accuracy lane in multiagent-orchestration-benchmark. A scan result that omits the flag is assessed as before.

A Gmail `Message-ID` the sender set is verified on the stored message and does identify it. Probe transports are GET-only and refuse any other path.

Readiness evidence for a reviewer, not an attestation.

In the production profile a side-effecting direct call without an idempotency key is refused. Found by running a real agent loop (agent-harness, context-runtime) against the gateway with a model that retries immediately after a timeout.

Replaces the fixed 15-minute credential lifetime.

No provider probes ship yet.
