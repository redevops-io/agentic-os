## Excluded on purpose, and why

**A person's decision.** The application declares these routes `human_only`: an agent never takes them, because the decision is what makes the action authorized. An agent may read the state, and propose where a tool says `propose`.

- agentic-privacy: `privacy.delete`
- edge-sentinel: `ban_ip`
- projects: `projects.confirm_sources`, `projects.connect_app_start`, `projects.connect_app`, `projects.policy_promotion_cycle`, `projects.today_approve`, `projects.today_batch`, `projects.today_reconcile`, `projects.today_simulate_outcome`

**Never a tool.** Deciding, executing and administering in the integrated products. A person does these in the product or in Projects, where the click mints the tenant-bound, single-use approval the product verifies: `quality.qa_approve_run`, `quality.qa_decide`, `quality.qa_delete_target`, `quality.qa_execute`, `quality.qa_register_target`, `security.sentinel_apply_network_policy`, `security.sentinel_approve`, `security.sentinel_decide`, `security.sentinel_execute`, `security.sentinel_manage_users`, `security.sentinel_retry`.

Every other operation below that agents cannot reach is **not yet offered**, not excluded.
